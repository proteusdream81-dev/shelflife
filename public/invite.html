ShelfLife is the book tracker that looks like your real shelf. Scan a barcode and the book lands where you can see it, import the library you already keep elsewhere, and hand a list to a friend when they ask what to read next.

What you can do with it

Add a book in seconds

Point the camera at the barcode, or search the title. The cover, author and details come with it.

Keep shelves, not spreadsheets

To read, reading, read. Group by series or format, and keep the private shelves private.

Read together

Share a shelf with your circle, follow what friends are reading, and talk about it in the comments.
