# Feature graphic brief (1024 × 500)

Use this when designing the Play Store feature graphic.

## Layout

- Canvas: **1024 × 500** px
- Background: deep plum `#1d0c1a` (match app chrome)
- Left or centre: app icon (`public/icon-512.png`) at ~180–220 px, rounded
- Wordmark: **ShelfLife** in Georgia or similar serif, cream `#f7ede6`
- Tagline under title: **Your reading life** in smaller gold `#f2a93b`
- Optional: faint shelf or book-spine silhouettes at low opacity; do not clutter

## Do not include

- Screenshots of other apps
- Placeholder ABN or fake business numbers
- “#1” ranking claims
- Competitor names (Goodreads / StoryGraph) on the graphic itself (OK in long description)

## Export

- PNG or JPEG, under 1 MB preferred
- No transparency on the final upload
